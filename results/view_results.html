🔧 Key Insight

LaMa works perfectly when mask fully covers the target region.

For subtitle removal, ensure masks are slightly larger than detected text.

# Example: Dilate mask before inpainting
import cv2
kernel = cv2.getStructuringElement(cv2.MORPH_ELLIPSE, (5,5))
mask_dilated = cv2.dilate(mask, kernel)

This small adjustment yields perfect inpainting on black background with white text.
